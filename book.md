### Chapter 1 

Opening my eyes, still tired from the restless night before, the yellow glow from my window touched my skin, my hair falling across my face, it's unkept from the movements of my rest. Blocking most of the warmth from the much-wanted sunlight.

I toss around till I'm awake enough to finally stand up, putting on my clothes. I step to the mirror and remove hairs that are becoming annoying, my face and head alike. I walk only a couple feet to the door and open it, surrounded by trees with the sun hitting my face and the wind blowing my hair only slightly.

This is it, this is what it's like to really be alive, I start to adventure around the surrounding area only for someone to start speaking to me. My daydream cut in half, and that someone is a lady, who is... *quite fat*, and she is asking for my seat on the train. Unworthy of my seating, I give her a disgusted look and hope she gets the message. She does not, and dares to ask again. I give another look. It starts to repeat, after an uncomfortable amount of disgusted faces I'm starting to think this *pig* has issues.
